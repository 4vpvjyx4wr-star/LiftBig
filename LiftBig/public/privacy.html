LIFTBIG

Privacy policy · Effective November 16, 2026

LiftBig is a workout journal. This page explains what the iPhone app and the website do with your information. LiftBig is a workout log, not medical advice, and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Data stored on your device

Your journal stays on the device where you use LiftBig. The iPhone app saves workouts, plans, and journal settings in the app’s private storage. The website keeps its copy in that browser’s local storage. Deleting the app removes the on-device journal. Clearing the website’s site data removes the browser copy.

You can export a backup file and import it later, including from the website into the iPhone app. No account is required to log workouts.

Optional account and sync

You can sign in with an email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple through Firebase Authentication. Sync is off by default. When you turn sync on, LiftBig copies your workouts, plans, and journal settings to Cloud Firestore so that journal can open on another phone where you sign in and turn sync on. The email address from that sign-in is stored so you can sign in again.

Deleting the account deletes the cloud copy of that journal. The journal already saved on the phone remains until you delete the app.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional and stays off until you turn it on. If you allow it, LiftBig uses body weight, height, and the workouts you log. You can turn this off in Settings. Turning it off does not delete workouts already saved in Apple Health.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS shows a notification at the time you chose. The rest timer can also notify you when rest ends, including on the Lock Screen. LiftBig does not control Apple’s Clock app.

Purchases

The iPhone app is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. This release does not include a subscription. LiftBig does not use the purchase to build an advertising profile.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not track you.

Children

LiftBig is not directed at children under 13.
